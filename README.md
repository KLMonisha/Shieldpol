# Shieldpol
ShieldPol is a privacy-first AI financial workspace for small organizations and early-stage businesses. It analyzes financial documents such as invoices, expense records, and bank statements while applying data minimization and tokenization to sensitive financial identifiers before information is processed by an AI model. The system is being developed as a hackathon project to demonstrate privacy-preserving AI workflows for financial data. The AI is used for document summarization, expense analysis, and preparation of financial information, and is not intended to provide personalized tax, legal, or investment advice.
